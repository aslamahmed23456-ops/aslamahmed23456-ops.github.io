The short version: CV Maker does not have an account system, a server, or a database. Your CVs, your photo and everything you type stay on your phone. We — the developer — cannot see any of it, because none of it is ever sent anywhere.

The one exception is advertising. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects data in order to serve them. That is described in full below.

1. Who this policy is from

This application is published by Zaroon Tahir (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use CV Maker – Resume Builder (“the app”).

2. Information we collect

We collect nothing. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, and no user accounts. It makes no network requests of its own; the only component in the app that contacts the internet is the Google Mobile Ads library described in section 4.

We have no server to receive your data, and no way to identify you.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your phone or tablet only. It is written to the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read.

WhatWhy
Your CV content — name, contact details, work history, education, skills and any other section you fill in To build and re-open your CVs
Your profile photo, if you add one To place it on your CV. Stored as a local file or an embedded image
Job applications you log — company, role, status and dates The application tracker
App settings — theme, language, text size, haptics To remember your preferences
Which templates and features you have unlocked So an unlock you earned is not lost

This information is never uploaded, backed up to us, synchronised, or shared with any third party by the app.

Deleting it

You can delete any CV inside the app. Deleted CVs go to a recoverable bin and can be permanently removed from there. Uninstalling the app removes everything it has stored. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on request — and nothing for us to restore, so please keep your own backups.

Backups you create

The app can export a backup file and export your CV as a PDF, Word document, plain text or JSON Resume file. These are handed to your device's own share or save dialog, and you choose where they go. Once a file leaves the app, it is governed by wherever you sent it — your email provider, cloud drive, or the recipient — not by this policy.

Note that Android's own system backup may include app data. That is a function of your device and Google account settings, not of this app.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app is free and is funded by advertising. It uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to display banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and connection information, such as device model, operating system version, language and coarse location derived from your IP address;
  • information about ads shown to you and whether you interacted with them.

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller under its own terms. We do not receive this data, and we cannot access it. Google's handling of it is described here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob

Your choices about ads

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form the first time it runs, and does not serve personalised ads without your consent. You can reopen that form at any time from Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy settings inside the app, and change or withdraw your choice.
  • On any device, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your system settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads). Deleting it prevents personalised advertising.
  • On iOS, the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking identifier is used, and you may decline.

Declining consent or tracking does not reduce what the app can do. Ads still appear, but they are non-personalised. No feature of the app is withheld because you declined.

5. Permissions the app asks for

Each of these is requested only at the moment you use the feature that needs it, and each is optional.

PermissionUsed forIf you decline
Photos / media Choosing an existing picture as your CV photo You can still build and export a CV without a photo
Camera Taking a new picture for your CV photo As above
Notifications Follow-up reminders you set yourself on a job application Reminders are not scheduled; nothing else changes
Advertising ID Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK You can reset or delete the identifier in system settings

Photos you select are copied into the app's own storage so your CV keeps working if the original is moved or deleted. They are not uploaded.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The app does not register for push notifications and has no push token, so no reminder passes through any server.

6. Read aloud

The app can read text aloud. It uses the text-to-speech engine built into your operating system. Whether that engine works entirely on-device depends on your device and its settings, which you control in your system's text-to-speech options. The app itself does not transmit the text anywhere.

7. Children

The app is intended for people seeking work and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children — we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Ad requests are configured with a maximum content rating of G (suitable for general audiences).

8. Your rights

Data-protection laws including the GDPR (EEA/UK) and the CCPA/CPRA (California) give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to certain processing.

For data held by this app those rights are satisfied directly and immediately by you: the data is on your device, you can view and edit all of it in the app, you can export it as a file, and you can delete it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are used in the CCPA/CPRA. For data processed by Google in connection with advertising, please exercise your rights with Google using the links in section 4, or by withdrawing consent in the app.

9. Data security

Your information is held in the app's private storage, which the operating system isolates from other applications. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no transfer to intercept and no database of ours to breach. The practical risks that remain are ordinary device risks: anyone with access to your unlocked phone can open the app, and any file you export is only as protected as the place you send it.
