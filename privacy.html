Privacy Policy

Mortgage & Loan Calculator · Last updated: 10 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mortgage & Loan Calculator mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

In short: The App works fully offline. Your calculations never leave your phone. We only receive personal details if you choose to submit the optional contact form — you can skip it and use every feature. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

The App is published by the developer of Mortgage & Loan Calculator (the "data controller"). For any privacy question or request, contact: zaroontahir1994@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

a) Information you choose to give us

The App shows an optional form. It can be skipped, and skipping does not limit any feature. If you complete it and tick the consent box, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional — you may leave it blank)
  • Your selected country, currency and language, plus your device platform and app version

This is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).

b) Information collected automatically

The App displays advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier, approximate location (derived from IP), and ad-interaction data in order to serve and measure ads. We do not receive your name or contact details from AdMob.

c) Information that never leaves your device

Everything you type into the calculators — home price, income, interest rates, loan amounts, saved scenarios — is stored only on your phone (local storage and an on-device database). We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. How we use your information

  • To contact you about mortgage-rate updates and product news, if you asked us to.
  • To let you back up and restore your saved scenarios, and to email you documents you request.
  • To display advertising, which keeps the App free.
  • To understand which countries and features are used, so we can improve the App.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or credit scoring.

4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Consent — for the contact details you submit and for personalised advertising. You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 7).
  • Legitimate interests — for basic, non-personalised app functionality, security, and abuse prevention.

5. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses these third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — Google Advertising Policy
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) — Firebase Privacy

On iOS you will be asked for permission before your device advertising identifier is used for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Declining does not stop you using the App; you will simply see non-personalised ads.

6. Sharing, disclosure and sale

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your contact details with lenders, brokers or data brokers.

We may disclose information only in these situations:

  • Service providers — Google (Firebase, AdMob), who process data on our behalf.
  • Legal obligation — where required by law, regulation or valid legal process.
  • Business transfer — if the App or our business is merged, acquired or sold, your information may be transferred to the acquiring party. That party will remain bound by this policy, and we will notify you of any material change.

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live (including the EU/UK under GDPR, and California under the CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent or object to processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

To request deletion: open the App and go to Saved Scenarios → Privacy & Data → Delete my data, or email zaroontahir1994@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will action verified requests within 30 days at no cost.

To remove the data held only on your phone, simply uninstall the App or clear its storage in your device settings.

8. Data retention

We keep the contact details you submit until you ask us to delete them, or until they are no longer needed for the purpose described above. Local calculation data lives on your device only, for as long as the App is installed.

9. International data transfers

Our servers are provided by Google Cloud and may be located in the United States or other countries. Where data is transferred out of the EU/UK, it is protected by Google's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored in Google Firebase with access rules that prevent the App (or anyone using it) from reading stored records. Only authorised administrators can access them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Not financial advice

All figures produced by the App are estimates for informational purposes only and do not constitute financial, tax or legal advice. Always consult a licensed mortgage professional before making a financial decision.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced within the App.
